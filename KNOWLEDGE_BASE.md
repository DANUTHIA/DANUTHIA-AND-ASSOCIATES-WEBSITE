# DANUTHIA & CO Architecture - Business Knowledge Base

This knowledge base is designed to train automated support systems and email responders to provide accurate, professional, and secure information to our clients.

## 1. Firm Overview
DANUTHIA & CO Architecture is a premier architectural design and project management firm dedicated to transforming visionary concepts into tangible, structurally sound realities. Our philosophy centers on balancing aesthetic elegance with functional durability. We believe that architecture goes beyond building structures; it is about crafting environments that elevate the human experience. Our core service offerings span the entire project lifecycle, from initial concept and feasibility studies to detailed design, engineering coordination, and full-scale project management.

## 2. Our Services
We offer a comprehensive suite of architectural and project management services, including:
*   **Architectural Design & Planning:** Creating innovative and functional designs tailored to client needs, ranging from residential to commercial master planning.
*   **Project Management (PM):** Overseeing project execution to ensure timelines, budgets, and quality standards are strictly met.
*   **Engineering & Structural Analysis:** Coordinating with top-tier engineers to ensure all designs are safe, compliant, and durable.
*   **Surveying & Site Analysis:** Conducting thorough site evaluations to inform design decisions and mitigate environmental risks.
*   **Financial Analysis & Budgeting:** Providing detailed cost estimations, budget tracking, and invoice management throughout the project lifecycle.
*   **3D Modeling & Visualization:** Offering immersive project models and visualizations to help clients experience their designs before construction begins.

## 3. Client Portal Guide
The DANUTHIA & CO Client Portal is the central hub for all project interactions. Here is how clients can maximize its use:
*   **Secure Access:** Clients access the portal using specialized, authenticated credentials verified by our secure system.
*   **Viewing Technical Reports & Blueprints:** Clients can navigate to their project dashboard to view and download all approved technical reports, CAD files, and blueprints.
*   **Financial Tracking:** Invoices and payment statuses are transparently tracked within the portal. Clients can view ledger updates in real-time.
*   **Project Milestones:** Clients can track real-time progress through interactive timeline updates from the project management team.
*   **Direct Communication:** The portal features a secure messaging system that connects clients directly with their assigned Project Manager and architectural team.

## 4. Our "Fortress" Security Model
We treat client data with the utmost sensitivity. Our "Fortress" security architecture ensures that all intellectual property, financial records, and personal information are heavily guarded:
*   **Strict Access Controls:** Data access is strictly compartmentalized. A client can only view details and documents explicitly tied to their project ID. We utilize a 'Default Deny' access model.
*   **Encrypted Document Vault:** All uploads, including technical reports, invoices, and blueprints, are stored in an encrypted cloud vault. Direct URL access is prohibited without active, verified authentication.
*   **Role-Based Data Isolation:** Our staff (Architects, Engineers, Financial Analysts) are granted access only to the systems and documents necessary for their specific roles, preventing internal data sprawling.
*   **Orphaned File Protection:** Automated cleanup protocols run silently to ensure that failed or rejected uploads do not linger in our systems or pose unauthorized access risks.

## 5. Administrative & Document Workflows
Efficient document management is critical to our project success:
*   **File Uploads:** Clients are encouraged to upload necessary site photos, inspirational documents, or compliance forms directly through the portal's secure file drop zone.
*   **Format Standards:** While we accept various formats, we recommend PDF for documents and high-resolution JPEG/PNG for imagery to ensure system compatibility.
*   **Review Protocols:** Once a document or technical report is submitted by our internal team, it undergoes a strict peer-review process before being published to the client-facing portal.
*   **Communication Protocols:** Official project approvals and change requests must be documented through the portal's messaging or document signing systems to maintain a single source of truth.
